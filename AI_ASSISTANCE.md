# AI assistance disclosure

The implementation and documentation of ckanext-metadata-assistant were developed by Björn Hagström with assistance from
Anthropic's Claude Code and OpenAI's Codex.

AI tools assisted with design discussion, code drafting, refactoring, review and documentation. Product decisions and
acceptance of the resulting work remain human decisions.

The human maintainer is the author, copyright holder and accountable project maintainer. AI systems are not listed as
authors or maintainers. Before a public release, the maintainer is expected to review the released diff, verify the
documented behavior and accept responsibility for correctness, security, licensing and provenance.

Contributors should disclose material generative-AI assistance in their pull request description. Disclosure does not
replace review, tests or the contributor's responsibility for the submitted material.
